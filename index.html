FAQS ABOUT PASSENGER

EVERYTHING BETWEEN YOU AND YOUR NEW LIFE

Do I need spacefaring experience?

Not even a little. Whatever you bring, whoever you might be, we'll match a placement to it, and train you for the rest. Nobody gets left behind for lack of experience.

Is this placement permanent?

Yes! This is a one-way ticket to a fresh start. Make sure you're ready to leave Earth behind for good before you apply.

What happens during the voyage?

You rest. Most colonists spend the crossing in cryo-sleep aboard the Chrono-Bay Wings, watched over by A.D.A.M. the whole way. Close your eyes on Earth, open them twenty years closer to Proxima. It really is that easy.

Can I bring family with me?

Every application is reviewed individually, so talk to a placement handler about your circumstances during your interview.

How do I begin my application?

One form is all it takes. Complete the application below and a placement handler will follow up to schedule your interview.
