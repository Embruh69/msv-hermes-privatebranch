OPEN ENROLLMENT

YOU DON'T NEED TO BE A SPACEFARER. YOU JUST NEED TO SAY YES, TODAY!

WHO WE'RE LOOKING FOR

If you've ever wondered what else is out there for you, you're in good company. Former security contractors and corrections officers who couldn't catch a break groundside. Doctors, teachers, engineers, and analysts whose expertise deserved so much better than Earth ever gave it. Dockworkers, cargo handlers, and maintenance crews who've always worked with their hands and never got the credit.

Every skill has a place on the Hermes, and every placement is a real second chance. If any of that sounds like you, PRS wants you on the manifest. We mean that.

WHAT THE ROLE INVOLVES

You only need to fill out a few simple forms: your name, your background, your skills. That's only one page of your new colonist record.

Beyond that, your story is yours to tell. We match your placement to what you already bring, train you for everything else, and pair you with a case handler who's personally invested in seeing you land on your feet and thrive.

REQUIREMENTS

Ready to commit? Here's exactly what it takes: you'll need to be 21 standard years or older to sign on independently, and prepared to see your full placement term through. No prior spacefaring or technical experience needed; that part's on us.

And like any voyage into the unknown, this one carries real risk we're not going to pretend away. By applying, you're telling us you understand that, and you're ready anyway.
