OUR MISSION

THOUSAND OF NEW BEGINNINGS. AND COUNTING.

THE SHIP

Meet the MSV Hermes: flagship vessel fleet and living proof that PRS builds to last. Every hull in this fleet is voyage-tested, ISO-certified, and held to the highest standard we're willing to put our name on.

Onboard, you are never alone: a licensed crew stands ready, and A.D.A.M. 7000, our shipboard intelligence, keeps every one of you safe, comfortable, and on schedule for arrival. Twenty years of trusted passage and thousands of new beginnings. That's the Hermes.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Proxima is waiting: roughly twenty years out at the Hermes' cruising speed, and the entire reason this program exists.

PRS is opening new colonist placements to anyone ready for a second beginning, and every one comes fully loaded: quarters aboard the ship, a placement built around what you already know, and a real, legal stake in the life waiting for you when you land. No spacefaring background required.

LIFE ABOARD THE HERMES

The most comfortable journey you'll ever take, where you can just spend it resting in cryo-sleep aboard the vessel, watched over by A.D.A.M. until it's time to wake. No long years to endure, no dead time to dread, just a night's rest between you and a new life.
